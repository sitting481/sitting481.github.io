Hello, my name is Chen Siting. I am currently pursuing an MSc in Operations Research and Business Statistics at Hong Kong Baptist University, having previously earned my Bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering from Nanfang College, Guangzhou. This combined background gives me both statistical thinking and technical implementation skills for data analysis.

During my internship at the Foshan Housing Provident Fund Management Center, I built an Excel-based case-flow dashboard to track administrative enforcement cases in real time. I integrated scattered case information into a structured dataset, which improved case-flow efficiency by about 20%, and I also reviewed over 250 case files for compliance and data integrity. This experience strengthened my ability to turn messy operational data into clear, actionable tracking tools.

I also have hands-on experience with SQL, database design, and visualization. At Zhonger Software, I designed database tables and wrote SQL aggregation scripts for multidimensional ticketing analysis across shift, station, route, and time dimensions. In another project, I used Spring Boot, Vue 3, MySQL, and ECharts to build an operations dashboard covering route performance, load factors, and passenger flow, and I developed automated reports that saved the operations team about five hours per week. These projects taught me to connect data analysis with business decision-making.

In a machine learning project, I preprocessed the UCI diabetes dataset and compared seven models using five-fold cross-validation. I selected logistic regression as the best model, achieving 81.66% test accuracy with balanced precision and recall. I also published a conference paper on AI-assisted medical diagnosis, which deepened my understanding of model evaluation, overfitting control, and robustness.

I am proficient in Python, SQL, Git, Java, and JavaScript, and I have a strong sensitivity to anomalies in data. I am seeking a data analyst role where I can use data cleaning, SQL analysis, visualization, and modeling to support data-driven business decisions. Thank you for your time and consideration.
